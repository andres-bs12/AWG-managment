## 5. Applying the public design system to staff

`docs/design-system.md` is explicitly public-only ("Staff UI: later"). The staff UI today uses a parallel `--staff-*` palette (`tokens.css:239-260`), `--font-staff` (IBM Plex Sans 400/600/700, loaded in `main.tsx`), `Button tone="staff"`, and many raw values: roughly 60 raw hex colours and 380 raw `px` lengths across 21 staff CSS modules, the heaviest being `NewSalePage.module.css` (7 hex / 87 px) and `AgendaPage.module.css` (6 / 69).

### 5.1 Reuse as is
- **Spacing** `--space-1..10`, **radius** `--radius-*`, **shadow** `--shadow-*`, **duration** `--dur-*`, focus ring (`--color-focus` + gap), and the CMP0 state matrix (hover, focus, pressed, disabled, busy, error).
- **Semantic roles**: `--color-error`/`-surface`, `--color-warning`/`-surface`, `--color-success`, `--color-disabled-*`, `--color-overlay`, `--color-control-border`.
- **C1 selection treatment** (2px selected boundary, tint, weight 600) for DayStrip chips, SegmentedControl, ChoiceList, colour/finish chips and Agenda selected slot. Today staff uses a filled dark block (`--staff-selected` #234c40).
- **Components**: `Button`/`ButtonLink` (keep the `tone="staff"` alias, mapped to the staff density), `Field`, `SegmentedControl`, `ChoiceList`, `HelpTip`, `InsetNote` (for "Customer details needed" and REVIEW alerts), `PublicState` pattern for loading/empty/error, `Alert`/error summary, the ornament swatch palette (§2.4) for colour dots, and native `<dialog>` (the cancel dialog already uses it; migrate `CompleteOrderDialog` to the same primitive).

### 5.2 What differs for staff
| Topic | Public | Staff |
|---|---|---|
| Font | Baloo 2 headings + Source Sans 3 | **IBM Plex Sans** everywhere (`--font-staff`). No display face, and headings use weight 600/700 Plex. Tabular numbers for times and money. |
| Density | 48px controls, 24–32px group gaps | Keep a **44px minimum hit area** (iPad, gloves), but tighter vertical rhythm: `--space-2`/`--space-3` between rows, 16px card padding. Add `--size-control-staff: 44px`. |
| Type scale | h1 1.75rem … | Add `--type-staff-{h1,h2,body,meta}`: h1 1.375rem, h2 1.125rem, body 1rem, meta 0.875rem. Never below 0.875rem (outdoor glare). |
| Colour | Warm surface, green primary | Neutral grey-green surface (`--staff-bg` becomes an alias `--color-staff-surface`). Same primary green and semantic roles. Keep the **slot status palette** (`--staff-slot-*`) as staff-only status tokens, each paired with a text label. |
| Selection | C1 | C1 (same), replacing the filled `--staff-selected`. |
| Layout | Single column, sticky bottom bar | Tablet first (iPad Air 11": 820×1180 / 1180×820). Two panes at ≥1024 (list + detail/side), a bottom tab bar under 1100 px (P1-25), primary actions in the lower half of the screen for thumb reach. |
| Copy | DE/EN, warm | English, short imperative, show amounts and times in buttons ("Mark paid · €62.98"). |
