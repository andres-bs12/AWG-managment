### Problem 1: Two Simultaneous Markets

#### 1. Data Model Impact & Migration Note
* **Can two markets share a date today?** **Yes in PostgreSQL, no in service validation.** [V1__baseline.sql](file:///Users/andresbejarano/dev/AWG%20Tracking/awg-tracking/src/main/resources/db/migration/V1__baseline.sql#L180-L181) and [MarketDay.java](file:///Users/andresbejarano/dev/AWG%20Tracking/awg-tracking/src/main/java/com/artwithgab/awg_tracking/model/MarketDay.java#L14) enforce `UNIQUE (market_id, date)`, so the table already supports a `MarketDay` per market per date.
* **What is missing on `OrderItem`?**
  * **Sale market** is already modeled: `OrderItem.soldOnDay` (`sold_on_day_id` FK to `market_day`, [OrderItem.java](file:///Users/andresbejarano/dev/AWG%20Tracking/awg-tracking/src/main/java/com/artwithgab/awg_tracking/model/OrderItem.java#L81-L83)).
  * **Pickup market** is **lost on save**: [OrderItem.java](file:///Users/andresbejarano/dev/AWG%20Tracking/awg-tracking/src/main/java/com/artwithgab/awg_tracking/model/OrderItem.java#L102-L109) stores only `deliveryKind`, `handoffDate` (`LocalDate`), and `pickUpTime` (`LocalTime`). On read, the backend guesses the market from `handoffDate`.
* **Migration (`V16__handoff_market_day.sql`):**
  * Add nullable `handoff_day_id uuid REFERENCES market_day(id)` to `order_item` (`@ManyToOne MarketDay handoffDay`).
  * Backfill existing rows: `UPDATE order_item oi SET handoff_day_id = md.id FROM market_day md JOIN market m ON m.id = md.market_id WHERE oi.delivery_kind = 'MARKET' AND oi.handoff_date = md.date AND m.kind = 'MARKET'`.
  * Keep `PaintingDay` (keyed by `date`) and `paintStart`/`paintEnd` global per date, since painting capacity belongs to Gaby's single workshop schedule.

#### 3. Minimal-Change Proposal
1. **Backend & Persistence:**
   * Remove the single-market-per-date check in `MarketService.saveMarket`; check `dayIsUsed` and pickup `hoursConflict` against `MarketDay.id`.
   * Add `soldOnMarketDayId` (optional) to `CreateSaleRequest` and persist `item.setSoldOnDay(...)` + `item.setHandoffDay(handoffDay)` from `row.marketDayId()`.
   * In `ViewMapper.handoffDay()`, return `Optional.ofNullable(item.getHandoffDay()).or(() -> index.marketDayOn(item.getHandoffDate()))`.
   * Add `markets: List<MarketDto>` to `AgendaDayDto` (all markets open that date) and `marketId` / `marketName` to `AgendaDeliveryDto`.
2. **Remembered Stall per iPad (`localStorage['awg-active-market-id']`):**
   * When >1 market exists, show a stall switcher pill in the staff header (`Stall: Rathausplatz ▾` → `Market A`, `Market B`, `All stalls`).
3. **Screen Behaviors:**
   * **New sale:** Default `soldOnMarketDayId` and "Today" pickup to the iPad's remembered stall. In the "Market day" `ChoiceList`, use `value={d.id}` (not `d.date`) and show `Market Name · Fri 28 Nov` so helpers can also book cross-stall pickups if requested.
   * **Agenda header & pickups:** Painting board stays unified. When 2 markets run on `date`, header shows the active stall or `Market A · Market B` with a filter pill (`This stall` / `All`) that filters the **Pickups & delivery** sidebar.
   * **Deliveries:** Default filter to the iPad's active market (`Market A` / `Market B` / `All` / `Vienna`), and display the specific market name (`Rathausplatz · 14:00`) instead of `"Market pickup"`.
   * **Track / Form / Invoices:** Driven by `ViewMapper.when()` and `InvoiceRenderer` via `item.getHandoffDay()`, automatically showing the right market name, stall number, and closing time.
   * **Web shop (`/buy`):** `CalendarIndex.marketDaysOn(date)` returns all open stalls; add `marketDayId` to `PickupDayDto` and `CheckoutRequest`, and key `BuyDateStep` radio cards by `entry.marketDayId`.

#### 4. Questions for the Owner
1. **Cross-stall pickups:** At Market A, should "Market day" pickup offer only Market A by default (with an option for Market B), or always show both markets together?
2. **Web shop pickups:** Can online customers choose pickup at either simultaneous market, or is online pickup limited to one primary stall?
3. **Numbered finished stock (`RM01`…):** Are numbered pieces split between the two stalls, or is the shared picker enough because the seller holds the physical piece ID in hand?
4. **Default painting hours on overlap days:** When two markets have different hours on the same date and no `PaintingDay` rule is saved, what default painting window should apply?
